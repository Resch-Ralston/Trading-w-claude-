# Quick Reference Guide - Ross Cameron Momentum Strategy

## TradingView Screener Criteria (Copy This!)

```
Market: US Stocks
Exchange: All

Required Filters:
├─ Price: $2.00 - $100.00
├─ Change % (Day): ≥ 3%
├─ Relative Volume: ≥ 2.0
├─ Volume: ≥ 500,000
├─ RSI (14): 50 - 80
├─ Price above EMA 9: Yes
├─ Price above EMA 20: Yes
├─ MACD Level: > 0
└─ Market Cap: $50M - $10B

Sort By: Change % (Descending)
```

## The 5 Pillars (Must Have All)

1. ✅ **Price**: $2 - $100
2. ✅ **Float**: Unlimited (any)
3. ✅ **Relative Volume**: 2x or higher
4. ✅ **Daily Gain**: 3% or higher
5. ✅ **Catalyst**: Recent news (check manually)

## Entry Checklist

- [ ] Stock shows up in screener
- [ ] Verified news catalyst exists (< 24 hours)
- [ ] Price above 9 EMA and 20 EMA
- [ ] RSI > 50 (bullish momentum)
- [ ] MACD line above signal line
- [ ] Volume > 500K shares
- [ ] Wait for pullback to VWAP or 9 EMA
- [ ] Enter on breakout confirmation

## Red Flags (Avoid These)

- ❌ No news catalyst
- ❌ Already up 50%+ (too extended)
- ❌ Low volume (< 500K)
- ❌ Wide bid/ask spread (> 5%)
- ❌ Recent reverse split
- ❌ Price below all moving averages
- ❌ RSI > 80 (overbought)
- ❌ Declining volume

## Green Flags (Look For These)

- ✅ Fresh news (< 24 hours)
- ✅ Up 3-15% (not too extended)
- ✅ Volume spike (2x+ average)
- ✅ Tight bid/ask spread
- ✅ Price consolidating after initial move
- ✅ Price holding above VWAP
- ✅ Higher lows forming

## Position Sizing Example

**Account Size**: $5,000

### Options Trade
- Risk per trade: 5% = $250
- If trade risks $1.00 per contract
- Max contracts: 2-3 contracts

### Shares Trade
- Risk per trade: 10% = $500
- Stock price: $10
- Stop loss: 5% = $0.50 per share
- Position size: $500 / $0.50 = 1,000 shares max

## Quick Formulas

**Risk/Reward Ratio**
```
R:R = (Target Price - Entry) / (Entry - Stop Loss)
Example: ($11 - $10) / ($10 - $9.50) = 2:1
```

**Position Size (Shares)**
```
Shares = Risk Amount / (Entry - Stop Loss)
Example: $500 / ($10 - $9.50) = 1,000 shares
```

**Relative Volume**
```
Rel Vol = Current Volume / 30-Day Average Volume
Example: 2M / 1M = 2x
```
